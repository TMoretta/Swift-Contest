    \begin{abstract}
        The management of contests, based on disjointed tools, is a fragmented and insecure process that compromises data integrity and real-time coordination.
        The state-of-the-art analysis confirmed a \textbf{market gap} for vertical solutions, motivating the design of \textbf{Swift Contest}: a secure, cross-platform, and \emph{end-to-end} software platform.

        To meet the requirements identified during the analysis, a modern technology stack was selected.
        The need for \emph{cross-platform} development led to the choice of the \textbf{Flutter} framework, while the requirement for a robust, relational backend prompted the adoption of \textbf{Supabase (PostgreSQL)}.
        Serverless logic was implemented strategically: \textbf{RPC Functions} in PL/pgSQL ensure the \textbf{atomicity of database transactions}, while \textbf{Edge Functions} in TypeScript handle \textbf{interactions with external APIs and Storage}.

        The system's design is founded on a secure architecture based on the \textbf{Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)}, where \textbf{Row Level Security (RLS) and a controlled API} work in synergy to mediate all data access.
        On the frontend, the \textbf{MVVM with BLoC} pattern ensures a separation of concerns, while on the backend, a \textbf{snapshot mechanism} guarantees voting integrity.
        A \textbf{hybrid authentication system} also supports secure access for ``Guest Jurors'' via anonymous authentication.

        The final project phase involved deploying its components on dedicated platforms: IaC migrations and serverless functions on the \textbf{Supabase} infrastructure, the web client on \textbf{Vercel} via CI/CD pipelines, and the Android artifact on \textbf{GitHub}. \textbf{This process led to the creation of a complete and working prototype}.
        In conclusion, Swift Contest not only validates the feasibility of the architectural approach but also stands as a \textbf{robust and scalable blueprint} for building secure collaborative applications, demonstrating how a modern technology ecosystem can fill a tangible market need.
    \end{abstract}
