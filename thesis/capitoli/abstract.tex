    \begin{abstract}
        The management of contests, based on disjointed tools, is a fragmented and insecure process that compromises data integrity and real-time coordination.
        The state-of-the-art analysis confirmed a \textbf{market gap} for vertical solutions, motivating the design of \textbf{Swift Contest}: a secure, cross-platform, and \emph{end-to-end} software platform.

        Swift Contest is designed to orchestrate the entire contest lifecycle, serving key roles such as the \textbf{Organizer}, the \textbf{Participant}, and the \textbf{Juror}.
        The system manages crucial phases from creation and configuration, to invitation and enrollment, work submission, structured voting, and results analysis.
        The system features two types of juries: ``\textbf{Appointed Juries},'' for jurors invited to formally enroll in the contest, and ``\textbf{Simple Juries},'' which allow access to voting via a shared token, also supporting unregistered users through an \textbf{anonymous authentication} mechanism.
        The application is developed in \textbf{Flutter} to ensure a consistent \emph{cross-platform} experience on Web and Android, with a robust backend based on \textbf{Supabase (PostgreSQL)}.

        The system's design is founded on a secure architecture based on the \textbf{Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)}, where \textbf{Row Level Security (RLS) and a controlled API} work in synergy to mediate all data access.
        On the frontend, the \textbf{MVVM with BLoC} pattern ensures \textbf{Separation of Concerns (SoC)}, while on the backend, a \textbf{snapshot mechanism} guarantees voting integrity.

        The final project phase involved deploying its components on dedicated platforms: IaC migrations and serverless functions on the \textbf{Supabase} infrastructure, the web client on \textbf{Vercel} via CI/CD pipelines, and the Android artifact on \textbf{GitHub}.
        In conclusion, Swift Contest not only validates the feasibility of the architectural approach but also stands as a \textbf{robust and scalable blueprint} for building secure collaborative applications, demonstrating how a modern technology ecosystem can fill a tangible market need.
    \end{abstract}
